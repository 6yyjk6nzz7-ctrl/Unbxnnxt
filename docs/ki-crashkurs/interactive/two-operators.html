01 Method

One workflow, run every day.

I built the whole operation as a single AI workflow and then ran it, every day, for a year. No team, no funding, nobody's permission. The leverage wasn't that AI wrote things for me — it was that one person could hold an entire business in their head and still ship.

It cost fifty-plus hours a week to do that alone. That's the number I'd most like to change, and it's the one thing a second operator actually fixes.

01

Leverage over labour

The limit stopped being hours and became judgement — knowing which output was good enough to ship and which would cost me later.

02

It compounds

Every workflow built once carries into the next company. Number two starts around month eight, not month zero.

03

The models keep moving

What took a week in January takes an afternoon now. Anything built on that curve gets cheaper to run the longer you run it.

04

The gap

What I don't have is a second person with taste and an outside eye. That has been the real bottleneck for months, not compute.
