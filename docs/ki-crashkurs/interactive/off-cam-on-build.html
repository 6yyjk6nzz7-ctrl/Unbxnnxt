What AI skills are actually worth right now

These are the real numbers, from the primary sources, with the caveats attached. The exciting ones and the sobering ones — because you'd spot a sales pitch in about four seconds.

What this actually means

The money isn't in operating the tool — 85% of German freelancers already do. It's in being the person who can tell whether the output is right. That takes subject knowledge plus the tool, and it's the one combination the data consistently rewards.

The honest reading of +62%

That figure compares job ads, not people. Nobody tracked the same person before and after learning AI, and it isn't controlled for seniority or sector. A large part of it just measures which kind of role mentions AI. Learning this does not hand you 62% more salary.

#1
Germany leads Europe

The one sentence version

In an overall shrinking German job market, the AI-adjacent part is one of the few that's growing — and it's growing fastest outside tech. That's the honest reason to look at this. It isn't a promise about your salary.

The thing I have to say out loud

I wanted this page to prove you could earn enough to walk away from your degree. I went looking for that number and it isn't there. In Germany in 2025, unemployment ran at 3.3% for graduates and 21.3% for people without a qualification — that gap didn't narrow in the AI era, it held.

So the honest pitch is smaller and, I think, better: this is worth doing alongside what you're already doing. Same evenings, no gamble. If it turns into money later, brilliant. If it just makes your coursework faster, that's already a win.
