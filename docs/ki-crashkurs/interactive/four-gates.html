rule 1

Milestones, never retainers

Freelance work is cut into deliverables with escrow release. A missed milestone costs one milestone, not a budget.

rule 2

Our QA, always

Everything external lands in the same review lane as our own output. Nothing ships because a stranger said it works.

rule 3

Legal is never DIY

The one line where the cheap option is the expensive option. A firm drafts it; we only decide what it must say.

03 Capex

Hardware at the gate, not before.

The flagship machine is in the plan — behind Gate 2, purchased from banked revenue. Until then the current M4 Max does the job it did all of year one. A bank reads that as discipline; we read it as free depreciation timing.

Set the workstation to the configured price on order day — it isn't public yet, so the model treats it as an assumption, released only if Gate 2 clears. Depreciation: straight-line, 3 years (GWG rules don't apply at this size).

05 Financing

Why a bank reads this one.

Banks don't decline AI — they decline plans with no repayment mechanics. This one leads with the thing they underwrite: staged drawdown, an itemised use of funds, a conservative case that still services debt, and covenants we propose ourselves.

Rate and term are assumptions, not quotes — set them to the Hausbank's offer in the meeting. Instruments to ask for, in order: ERP-Gründerkredit StartGeld (KfW, via any Hausbank, 80% Haftungsfreistellung), a Bürgschaftsbank guarantee if collateral is thin, plain Kontokorrent only for the working-capital sliver.

covenant · proposed

Gate spend-lock

No Phase-1 drawdown before Gate 1 evidence is delivered to the bank. We propose the covenant; they usually soften it.

what they'll ask

The file

Selbstauskunft, last tax assessment, this dossier printed, the year-one account statements, and the Gewerbe registration.

what kills it

What we avoid

Hockey-stick-only projections, "the market is huge", uncosted plans, and asking for the maximum instead of the trough ×1.2.

fallback

If the bank says no

The gates don't change — the timeline stretches. Phase 1 self-funds from Phase 0 revenue at roughly half speed. The plan survives its own rejection.
