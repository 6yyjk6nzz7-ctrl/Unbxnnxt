01 Architecture

Where the queue actually forms.

Generation was never the bottleneck — it fans out as wide as I want. Everything serialises at the one stage that can't be parallelised by spending money: a human deciding whether the output is good enough to ship.

Pipeline · one operator vs twolive
01

Generation is elastic

Widening the fan-out costs money and nothing else. It has never been the reason something shipped late.

02

Review is not

Judgement doesn't parallelise across one person. Every draft waits for the same pair of eyes.

03

Rework compounds it

A rejected draft re-enters the same queue. That's why the honest gain is sub-linear, not double.
